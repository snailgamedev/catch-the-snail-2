CATCH THE SNAIL

HOW TO PLAY
Move your mouse over a snail to catch it. If any part of your crosshair touches a snail, it counts.
Each wave adds more snails. Catch them all to clear the wave.
Watch out! Snails can hit you with football and basketball moves that freeze your cursor.
Rarity matters. Common (easy) to Celestial (nearly impossible).
Earn awards by catching snails, surviving moves, clearing waves, and more.

HOW TO PLAY (MULTIPLAYER)
Catcher: Move cursor over snails to catch them. Same as singleplayer.
Snail: WASD/arrows or mouse/touch to move. Press E for your type ability. Press SPACE for a sport move.
Mobile: Drag to move. Green button = ability. Red button = sport move.
Snails get 5 seconds head start before the catcher is released!
